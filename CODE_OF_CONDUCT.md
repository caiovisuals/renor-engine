# Code of Conduct

Renor is a memory engine for people's own notes and their AI tools. Treat everyone who works on it with the same care the engine is built to give a person's memory: be direct, be specific, and assume good faith.

## Standards

- Critique code and designs, not people. Say what is wrong and why, with evidence.
- Keep discussion on the work. No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or public shaming.
- Do not post private data: other people's notes, credentials, personal paths, or anything from a memory you do not own.
- Disagree in the open when the evidence requires it, and accept a decision once it is made.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and any other project space, and to anyone representing the project in a public space.

## Reporting

Report a conduct concern privately through GitHub's private advisory form: <https://github.com/renorai/renor-engine/security/advisories/new>. Do not post it as a public issue. Include what happened and where. Reports are read by the maintainer, and reporters are not named in any public record without their consent.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not this process.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides the response, proportionate to the harm: a private warning, removal of a post, a temporary block from project spaces, or a permanent one for repeated or serious conduct. The maintainer may act on behalf of the project without the reporter's agreement when a risk to others is clear.
