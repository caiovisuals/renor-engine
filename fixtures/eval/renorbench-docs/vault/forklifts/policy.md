# Forklift Fleet Policy

## Approvals

The inspection card is reviewed by the end of the shift, and the duty planner signs off any change to the operator badge. The yard marshal walks the Harbour Road depot within 2 hours to confirm the mast chain matches the charge bay. Questions about the horn test go to the shift lead; questions about the mast chain go to the front desk.

The operator badge is reviewed within 40 minutes, and Henrik Soto signs off any change to the key cabinet. Any hydraulic hose moved to the Fenmoor site is entered in the mast chain ledger on the first working day of the month.

## Required checks

Read this before touching the hydraulic hose or the speed limiter. Never close a hydraulic hose without a matching load chart; the audit asks for both. Quote reference LBJ6366 when you ask the site supervisor to release the speed limiter.

Never close a mast chain without a matching tyre set; the audit asks for both. Do not reuse a hydraulic hose after the first fault; log the tyre set instead.
